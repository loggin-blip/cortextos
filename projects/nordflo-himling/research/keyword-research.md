## Content Gap Analysis

Norwegian users ask questions no one answers well:

1. **Pris-guider** — "Hva koster himling?" has no authoritative Norwegian page. Create a detailed m2-pris calculator page.
2. **Sammenligning** — No content compares systemhimling vs strekkhimling vs gips. A comparison guide would capture mid-funnel traffic.
3. **Regelverk** — TEK17 brannkrav + akustikk-krav for naeringsbygg is poorly covered in plain language.
4. **Vedlikehold** — "Hvordan rengjore/vedlikeholde himling" has zero good Norwegian results.
5. **Prosjektbilder** — Competitors lack before/after case studies with Oslo-specific projects.

## Competitor Landscape

Top-ranking domains: oslohimlinger.no, amberbygg.no, aspira.no, glava.no, bygghjemme.no. Most have thin service pages with little educational content — strong opportunity for content-led SEO.

## Local SEO Actions

1. **Google Bedriftsprofil** — category "Himlingsmontør" or "Bygginnredning", complete NAP, photos of Oslo-prosjekter
2. **Gule Sider + Proff.no** — consistent listings with exact same name/address/phone
3. **Anmeldelser** — target 15+ Google reviews within 3 months; respond to all
4. **Lokale landingssider** — separate pages for "himling Oslo", "himling Asker Barum", "himling Follo" etc.
5. **Schema markup** — LocalBusiness + Service structured data on every page
